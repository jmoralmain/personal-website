Ten weeks in, almost nobody counts yet

The startup page values players by points above replacement: how far they outscore the 24th QB, 30th RB, 42nd WR or 14th TE. By that measure this class has produced very little so far. Only three rookies are above the line. That is normal for rookies in October and November, and it means points above replacement can't separate most of these picks yet.

So this page leads with a softer number. Points over slot compares each rookie's 2026 points with the average of the seven picks around him (his own pick and three on each side). It rewards a player who is contributing at all, even below replacement. Points above replacement and its own value over slot are shown next to it everywhere.

Where rookies played for their teams

The rebuilding teams are leaning on their rookies. deathreaper439 (1-9) has started rookies 15 times, the most in the league, and mynameisuser (2-8) is next with 13. The top three teams by record, Perriconejp (9-1), Bengraz03 (8-2) and djklotzshotz (8-2), have started rookies 7 times between them.

Who kept their picks

31 of 36 rookies are still with the team that drafted them. Five were traded: Zachariah Branch was moved hours after the draft, and Stribling, Drew Allar, Fernando Mendoza and Antonio Williams went in weeks 1 and 2. Of the five, only Williams is a weekly contributor, starting 7 times for masonmay.

Traded picks: who owned them, who made them

Half the board changed hands before the draft: 18 of 36 picks were made by a team other than the original owner. The trades that moved the most draft capital are going like this.

  • mynameisuser won the #8 for #17 swap. It turned #8 (bought from Perriconejp for Trey Benson and James Conner) into #17 plus Luther Burden III. #17 became Boston, the top rookie. #8 became Simpson, who hasn't played.
  • DrKnockerzz's James Cook III trade is paying off. It sent James Cook III and its 2027 1st to Bengraz03 for Travis Etienne Jr., #5 and #7. #7 became Concepcion. #5 became Mendoza, who was then flipped for Adams and a 2028 1st.
  • deathreaper439 bought well at #12. It gave Sam Darnold to GAVINKIRK for that pick, and Coleman has scored 62.9 points. It also sold Drake London after the draft for Allar plus djklotzshotz's 2027 and 2028 1sts.
  • Bengraz03 traded out of the draft and is 8-2. It made one pick (#23) after dealing three of its own: #4 in the Ja'Marr Chase trade, #16 for Lamar Jackson and #28.

Open questions

  • How long is Love out? The #1 pick was a weekly starter twice in five games before the injury. The data shows IR and nothing about a return date.
  • Is Boston's slide a slump or a role change? His snap share barely moved (92% to 88%), so the opportunity is still there. That points to a slump.
  • Are the late-season risers real? Johnson, Concepcion, Makai Lemon and Omar Cooper Jr. each had three starter weeks in weeks 6–10. Seven more games will show whether those roles hold.
  • When do the quarterbacks play? Four QBs went in this draft and have 16.5 points combined. Mendoza's full game in week 10 is the first real start. In superflex they are long-term bets, and this page can't judge them yet.
  • Do the tight ends develop? Kenyon Sadiq (TE28) and Raridon (TE32) are playing, but neither is close to the 84.3-point TE replacement line. Tight ends usually take longer.
  • Did the trades for future 1sts pay? deathreaper439 added two future 1sts by selling Drake London, and DrKnockerzz added TannerMenning's 2028 1st by selling Mendoza. Those picks will be judged in later drafts.

How this was measured. Data from the Sleeper public API, pulled 18 November 2026: the 2026 rookie draft (3 rounds, 12 teams, linear order, 36 picks), every player's weekly NFL stats for 2026 weeks 1–10, scored with the league's settings (superflex, full PPR). Points above replacement (PAR) is a player's 2026 total minus that of the 24th QB (105.0), 30th RB (75.9), 42nd WR (81.3) or 14th TE (84.3) through week 10, floored at 0. These are the same levels the startup page uses.

Value over slot compares a pick with the mean of the seven picks centered on it (three on each side, fewer at the ends of the draft). It is shown two ways: on raw 2026 points (points over slot, used for grades and the default board) and on PAR. A weekly starter finish is a top-24 QB, top-24 RB, top-36 WR or top-12 TE week by points. Games played uses Sleeper's games-played flag, not its active flag, which is also set for players on IR. Snap share comes from Sleeper's offensive snap counts.

Caveats: ten games is a small sample, and a rookie's value is mostly in future seasons this page can't see. A pick's value follows the player wherever he went, so it measures the pick, not what the team did with him later. Starts and lineup data come from the league's weekly matchups. Depth chart and injury status are Sleeper's values on 18 November.
