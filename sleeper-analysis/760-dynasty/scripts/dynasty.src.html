Did the draft decide the league?

Mostly, yes. Across the 12 teams, draft value and total wins since 2024 line up closely (correlation 0.77). Every 100 points of draft value is worth about one win over three seasons. Teams above the line have won more than their draft predicts; teams below it have won less.

The grades

Each pick is worth its points above replacement: how much it outscored a freely available player at its position each season. A season below replacement counts as zero. Value over slot compares that with the average of the 21 picks around it, so a top-five pick only gets credit for beating what top-five picks usually return.

"Starter hits" counts picks with at least one starter-grade season (top 24 QB, top 24 RB, top 36 WR, top 12 TE). "Still here" counts startup picks on the team's roster today. Records are regular season, and 2026 runs through week 10.

Best and worst picks

What the board says about drafting here

  • Running backs were the value play. RBs taken in rounds 1–5 beat their slot by 70 points on average. Wide receivers there came in 39 under, and tight ends 58 under.
  • Early tight ends were a trap. Of the five taken in the first five rounds, Sam LaPorta and Dalton Kincaid have almost nothing above replacement since 2024.
  • The 2024 rookie class underdelivered. The 58 rookies taken in the startup came in 22 points under their slot on average. Marvin Harrison Jr. (#13), Rome Odunze (#47) and Jonathon Brooks (#59) were the costliest. Drake Maye (#57) and Brock Bowers (#44) were the hits.
  • Quarterbacks went early and held their value. Nine of the 12 first-round picks were QBs, and as a group the QBs taken in rounds 1–5 landed almost exactly on their slot (8 under). The misses were concentrated: Anthony Richardson, Tua Tagovailoa and Kyler Murray.

Data from the Sleeper public API, pulled 18 November 2026: the 2024 startup draft (25-round snake, 300 picks), every player's weekly stats for 2024 and 2025 (weeks 1–17) and 2026 (weeks 1–10), scored with the league's own settings (superflex, full PPR; unchanged across all three seasons).

Replacement level is that season's points total of the 24th QB, 30th RB, 42nd WR and 14th TE. A pick's value counts every season, whoever owned the player, so it measures the pick itself rather than what the team later did with it. It also can't see the future: a young player's value from 2027 on isn't counted. Records come from each season's final standings.
