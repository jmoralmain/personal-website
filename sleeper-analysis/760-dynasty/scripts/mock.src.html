760 Dynasty 2027 Mock Draft

A three-round mock of next year's rookie draft. The order and pick owners come from the Draft picks tab, projected from current standings. The prospects and their order come from a current consensus superflex ranking. Each team's choice leans toward the positions where its roster is thinnest or oldest.

The mock

"Board" is the player's spot in the consensus ranking, and "vs board" shows how early (−) or late (+) he goes here. A pick is marked best available when the team takes the top-ranked player left, fills a need when it passes on him for a position its roster is short at, and roster fit otherwise.

What each roster needs

Need compares each team's starters with the rest of the league: its best two quarterbacks (it's superflex), three running backs, three receivers and top tight end, by points above replacement per game this season. Red marks a position well below the league, and blue one well above. Starters who are old for their position add to the need, since this is a dynasty league.

Need is in standard deviations from the league average, plus 0.5 when the starters' average age is 32+ at QB, 27+ at RB, 29+ at WR or 30+ at TE. Hover a cell for the players behind it. Records are 2026 through week 10.

Every team's class

How this was made. Prospects and their order come from The Big Board's 2027 superflex rookie rankings (12-team PPR, updated 17 November 2026), which match the order in other current lists: Jeremiah Smith first, then a deep quarterback group. Each pick takes the prospect with the best mix of that ranking and the team's need: the ranking's own value score, raised by up to about 40% for the team's thinnest position. After a pick, that team's need at the position drops.

It's a projection, not a prediction of any one manager. The real draft order depends on the last seven weeks, the prospects' values will move with their NFL landing spots in April, and managers trade picks. Roster needs use 2026 production only.
