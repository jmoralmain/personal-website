Data from the Sleeper public API, pulled 18 November 2026, scored with the league's own settings (superflex, full PPR). Every value is points above replacement: what a player scored beyond a freely available player at his position (24th QB, 30th RB, 42nd WR, 14th TE).

The four parts measure different things and are added as they are. Draft values count everything a drafted player produced, whoever owned him later, so they grade the pick. Trade values count only what players produced in the new team's starting lineup, minus what the outgoing assets produced for their new teams, so a drafted player who was traded away still credits the drafter and debits the trade. Future picks for 2027 and later have no value yet and appear only in the outlook. 2026 runs through week 10.
