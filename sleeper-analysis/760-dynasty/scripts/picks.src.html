What each team holds

Every pick each team owns, with its source and value.

How this was measured. Pick ownership comes from Sleeper's traded-picks list for the league on 18 November 2026, and the trade history from every completed trade since 2024. The league's rookie drafts have three rounds, so each team starts with nine picks across 2027–2029.

Pick values use the curve fitted to the 2025 rookie draft's 36 picks, from the 2025 rookie draft tab: expected points above replacement ≈ 105.6 − 29.9 × ln(pick number), floored at zero. It's built from one class, so treat the values as a rough guide to how much more an early pick is worth than a late one. 2027 slots are projected from 2026 standings through week 10.
