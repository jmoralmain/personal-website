Do trades explain who beat their draft?

Partly. The startup-draft page found that draft value predicts wins well, with a few teams far off the line. Net trade value lines up with those gaps, though less tightly now that future picks count (they haven't won anyone a game yet): across the 12 teams the correlation between net trade value and wins above or below the draft's prediction is 0.63. In this league about 71 points of scoring has been worth one win over three seasons, so the table converts trade value into wins at that rate.

"Draft predicts" uses the same fit as the draft page: wins over 2024–26 against startup value over slot. "Trades worth" is net trade value divided by 71 points per win, the slope of wins on total points scored across the 12 teams. Treat it as a rough conversion.

  • masonmay: the trades are the story. Its +408 net covers its 4.9-win surplus, and the timing fits. Its trades added 86 in 2024 and 295 in 2025, its two 9-5 playoff seasons. In 2026 they are −36, and it is 2-8. Puka Nacua alone is worth 408 of the net.
  • mynameisuser: trades explain little. By season the trades were −70 in 2024 and +57 in 2025, so the 2-12 collapse in 2025 was the roster, not trading. The 2026 losses (−202) are real, mostly Ja'Marr Chase, Michael Wilson and Bhayshul Tuten producing for Bengraz03, but the future picks it got back are worth about as much, so the net is −5.
  • Trading more did not help by itself. Of the four busiest teams, masonmay (17 trades, +408) and Bengraz03 (13, +343) gained, mynameisuser (14, −5) broke even and GAVINKIRK (13, −406) lost. The correlation between number of trades and net value is 0.24.

Did a trade win a title?

No. Both finals were blowouts: KDEMPSTER15 won the 2024 final by 78 points and GAVINKIRK won the 2025 final by 88. Trades shaped the seasons around those games, though.

  • 2024, KDEMPSTER15. Its one trade that season brought Derrick Henry from ryanoleson5 in week 11 (#11). Henry added 41 above replacement the rest of 2024, 27 of it in the playoffs and 16.5 in the final. The runner-up, DrKnockerzz, made no trades in 2024.
  • 2025, GAVINKIRK. It won in spite of its trades. Players it traded for produced 194 above replacement for it in 2025, led by DK Metcalf (69), Nico Collins (69) and Sam Darnold (42). Players and picks it had traded away produced 507 for other teams that year, led by Drake Maye (179 for ryanoleson5) and George Pickens (112 for Bengraz03). Its traded-in players scored nothing above replacement in the final.
  • 2025, runner-up TannerMenning. Its best trade, Davante Adams (#15), produced 104 above replacement for it in 2025 and helped carry it to the final.

Every trade

All 60, oldest first. The higher-value side is listed first. Picks show the player they became, and later picks are marked as future picks and counted at their expected value.

How this was measured. Data from the Sleeper public API, pulled 19 November 2026: every completed trade in the 2024, 2025 and 2026 leagues, weekly lineups, and every player's weekly stats scored with the league's own settings (superflex, full PPR, unchanged across seasons). Weeks counted: 2024 and 2025 weeks 1–17, 2026 weeks 1–10.

What a team received is worth the points above replacement its new players scored in weeks they were in its starting lineup, from the trade's week until they left the roster. Replacement is that week's 24th-best QB, 30th RB, 42nd WR and 14th TE among players who played, the same levels the draft page uses. A week below replacement counts as zero. A traded pick is credited to the team that used it, for the player it drafted, from that draft on. A pick that was traded again before the draft is worth nothing to the team that passed it on; its value shows up in the later trade. A pick for 2027 or later counts at its expected value from the Draft picks tab: the points above replacement a rookie taken there typically produces over his first season and a half, using the 2027 order projected from the standings and a round average for 2028 and 2029. Net value is what a team received minus the value of what it sent away.

Caveats. Only starts count, so a player held on the bench as insurance earns nothing. The 37 picks for 2027 and later are worth 1,244 at expected value, which is a projection, not production; the 2027 slots will move with the standings. The 2026 rookies have had ten weeks. 2026 is through week 10. A trade is "too close" when the margin is under 15 points and "too early" when most of its assets are 2026 rookies or future picks. One trade also moved $30 of FAAB, which is not valued.
