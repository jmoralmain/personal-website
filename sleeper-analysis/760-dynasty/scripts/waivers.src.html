The waiver ledger

Each team's adds and drops added up. Net is what its pickups produced minus what its drops gave the teams that picked them up next.

"10+ pickups" counts adds that produced at least 10 starter points above replacement for the team. FAAB applies to 2025 and 2026 ($100 a season); 2024 used rolling waiver priority with no bidding. "Per $1" is points above replacement from paid claims for each FAAB dollar spent.

How this was measured. Every completed waiver claim and free-agent add in the 2024, 2025 and 2026 leagues, from the Sleeper public API, pulled 18 November 2026. Weeks counted: 2024 and 2025 weeks 1–17, 2026 weeks 1–10, scored with the league's own settings.

An add is worth the points above replacement the player scored in weeks he was in the adding team's starting lineup, from the add until he left the roster, including later seasons. Weekly replacement is that week's 24th QB, 30th RB, 42nd WR and 14th TE among players who played, the same levels as the Trades tab. A drop costs what the next team to add that player got from him by the same measure; if the dropping team re-added him first, the drop costs nothing. That way each point is charged once. Kickers and defenses aren't rostered in this league.
