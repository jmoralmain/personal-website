Paying over the next bid

FAAB auctions are blind, so the winner pays their own bid, not one dollar over second place. The gap between the winning bid and the best losing bid is money spent for nothing. Every survivor gave away roughly a third of its budget this way.

Started or benched

A pickup only helps in the weeks he's in the lineup. Across their waiver claims, the four teams got 74–87% of their pickups' points into the starting lineup. None of them started a claimed player in a week he didn't play.

masonmay and Love Hurts left the most on the bench, 129.0 and 128.6 points. masonmay's biggest leak was Patrick Mahomes (51.6 bench points). Love Hurts' was spread out: KC Concepcion Jr. scored 37.8 and never started, Mike Evans scored 25.0 from the bench, and Luther Burden III scored 18.6.

KDEMPSTER15 had the opposite problem. It started Tyler Warren in all three of his games at 5.5 points a game, and benched Caleb Williams in three of his four (47.2 points). TannerMenning used its pickups best, starting players for 87% of their points. Most of what it left on the bench (39.1 of 59.1 points) came from Jordan Love, its $0 quarterback.

Who played it best

Love Hurts, by less than the raw totals suggest. Its pickups scored 701.6 starter points, the most of any survivor. Some of that comes from a weak draft that forced pickups into the lineup: Love Hurts' drafted players produced 452 starter points, against 649 to 966 for the other three. The fairer measure is points above replacement: what a pickup scored in the weeks he started, minus what a free-agent-level player at his position scored that week.

On that measure Love Hurts leads at the base line, 226.9 to masonmay's 191.3, and it pays far less for its production than anyone at every line. Hold pickups to a strict starter bar and masonmay and TannerMenning edge ahead, which keeps the margin narrow. It isn't one lucky claim, either. Take away each team's two best pickups and Love Hurts is still level with masonmay and ahead of TannerMenning.

Pickup points above replacement, started weeks only

Replacement is that week's score of the Nth-best player at the position among those who played. Strict: QB10, RB20, WR20, TE10. Base: QB13, RB30, WR30, TE13. Loose: QB18, RB45, WR45, TE18. Covers waiver claims and free-agent adds.

  1. Love Hurts

    The most production for the least money. Kyren Williams ($211) has started all nine weeks since his claim. Joe Burrow ($2) has a median weekly finish of QB2, and Christian McCaffrey ($100) is scoring 34.5 a game. That's $3.56 of FAAB per point above replacement, against $7.39 or more for everyone else. The weak spots are real but fixable. Its buys produce less per start than TannerMenning's. Thirteen bids failed because the roster was full, which cost it Josh Jacobs, George Kittle and Matthew Golden as the high bidder. It paid 38% of its spend above the best losing bid. And it left 128.6 claim points on the bench.

  2. masonmay

    The best players, at a high price. Ja'Marr Chase ($426) and Trey McBride ($249) became weekly starters, and no team got more from free pickups, with Drake Maye the best of them. Its pickups lead the four survivors at the strict starter bar. It took $4,346 in bids to land $996 of claims, though, and with $4 left it's out of the auction for good.

  3. TannerMenning

    The best pickups per start, but only two that cost anything. Puka Nacua ($700) and Jahmyr Gibbs ($280) took 98% of the budget, at $199 and $98 above the next-best bids. Its best pickup was free: Jordan Love, claimed for $0 in week 1. Running out of money has already cost it Tee Higgins. Its $200 bid in week 9 failed, and Love Hurts got him for $60.

  4. KDEMPSTER15

    Last at every line. It overpaid by the most ($394, or 41% of its spend), paid $66 for Brock Bowers when nobody else bid, and got 2.2 starter points from a $109 Jeremiyah Love claim. It also kept starting Tyler Warren at 5.5 points a game. Lamb, Cook and Bowers have yet to play a week for it, so the bill for its last big run is still open.

Who wins it all

Three weeks remain if the league keeps one chop a week: weeks 11 and 12 each cut a team, and the last two meet in week 13.

The pick

KDEMPSTER15, with Love Hurts close behind

KDEMPSTER15 has the strongest roster left: Josh Allen, CeeDee Lamb, Amon-Ra St. Brown, James Cook, Bucky Irving and Brock Bowers. It's the least likely team to be chopped this week (15%), the most likely to reach the final (64%), and it has the best week-12 projection at 163 points. The worst waiver team is the favorite because its draft and its last-week haul of stars carry it.

Love Hurts is the real threat. Burrow (35.1 points a game over weeks 8–10) and McCaffrey (34.5) are the hottest pair on any roster, and if they stay that hot, Love Hurts is the favorite. A Love Hurts–KDEMPSTER15 final would be close to even. KDEMPSTER15 loses Bowers and Derrick Henry to byes in week 13, which drops its projection to 146, against Love Hurts' 149.

TannerMenning has the hardest week 11: Puka Nacua is on IR, and Jaxon Smith-Njigba and Drake London are on bye. If it survives, its week-12 projection is second-best. masonmay is the most likely team to be chopped this week, at 35%.

Hold this loosely. Tested on weeks 4–10 using only what it would have known beforehand, the same model picked the chopped team in 3 of 7 weeks. On average it gave the team actually chopped 27%, about twice a random guess. Last week it made KDEMPSTER15 a 61% chop favorite, and KDEMPSTER15 finished second.

Title odds

20,000 simulated finishes, run once weighting each player's last three weeks double and once weighting his whole season evenly; the odds are the average of the two. Each player's weekly score is drawn from his own 2026 game log in this league's scoring, and every team starts its best lineup by expected points. Byes come from the NFL schedule and injuries from Sleeper's reports as of 18 November 2026: IR is out, Out probably misses week 11, and Questionable usually plays. After each chop, teams claim the chopped roster in FAAB order with their real budgets. Leaving waiver claims out moves no team by more than 4 points, since the chopped rosters' best players rarely beat the starters these four already have.

Projected lineup points and key absences

Projection is the expected score of the best available lineup, with every week counted evenly and before any waiver claims. Absences list players averaging 10+ points who are on bye or likely out. Injury statuses will change before kickoff.

Track record · the same model on weeks already played

For each week, the model used only game logs from earlier weeks and each team's roster that week. It counted a player as available if he actually played, which gave it perfect injury news, so real forecasts will do worse. Weeks 1–3 are left out because there wasn't enough history yet.

Data from the Sleeper public API, pulled 18 November 2026. Every completed and failed waiver bid from weeks 1–11 is included. Points use the league's own scoring settings (full PPR); recomputed totals match Sleeper's matchup scores on all 1,323 player-weeks.

"Pickup points" credits a player to a team only in weeks he was on that team's roster and in its starting lineup, after the add. Claims from the week-10 waiver run are marked pending: they haven't played a scored week yet.
