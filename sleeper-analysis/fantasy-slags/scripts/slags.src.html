The grades

A pick is worth its points above replacement through week 10. Value over slot subtracts what a pick at that spot is expected to return, from a smooth curve fitted to all 264 picks. The curve expects about 112 at #1, 65 at #10, 51 at #20 and 19 at the end of round 8, reaching zero around pick 240, so a first-round pick has to produce a lot to grade well.

Grade cutoffs on value over slot: A 150+, B+ 75+, B 25+, C −25+, C− −75+, D −125+, D− −175+, F below. "Above replacement" counts picks with any value this season. "Still here" counts startup picks on the team's roster today.
