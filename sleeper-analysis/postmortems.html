Chopped Run Post-Mortems

Ten teams have been chopped, one a week. This reviews each of them: how they went out, whether a better lineup would have saved them, and which decisions sank them. Then it names the worst manager of the ten.

Worst manager
hnestande
  • The worst lineups of anyone chopped. It scored 81% of what its best possible lineup would have, leaving 19.2 points a week on the bench.
  • An avoidable chop. In week 4 it started Ladd McConkey and Rashee Rice for 0.0 points combined, with Malachi Fields (13.2) and Pat Freiermuth (10.7) on the bench. Its best lineup would have cleared the next-lowest team by 13.7.
  • It cut its best player. It dropped Patrick Mahomes, its 8th-round pick, in week 1. Mahomes scored 85.6 points during hnestande's four weeks, and 181.4 for the season.
  • A thin draft. Its 14 picks rank 11th of 14 in points produced, and it started Puka Nacua in week 2 when he didn't play.

The scorecard

The grade ranks the ten chopped teams on the three things a manager fully controls: lineups (share of their best possible score they actually scored), the chop (how avoidable it was), and the draft (points their 14 picks produced this season, whoever ended up rostering them). Each is ranked 1 (worst) to 10, and the lowest total is the worst manager.

Ties are broken on lineup efficiency. DrKnockerzz played one week, so its lineup and chop numbers rest on a single game. For scale, the four survivors set lineups at 85–90%. Love Hurts and masonmay are both at 85%, below eight of the ten chopped teams, so lineups aren't everything.

How they spent their FAAB

All four survivors have spent at least 90% of their $1,000. Four of the ten chopped teams went out with 89% or more of theirs still unspent. The six that did spend mostly paid early and paid big for players who then outlasted them.

Worst use of FAAB
GAVINKIRK
  • $18.89 per starter point, the worst rate of the ten. It spent $803 and got 42.5 points from those players in its lineup.
  • One bid was 74% of the budget. It paid $740 for Chris Olave in week 3, $335 more than the next-best bid (TannerMenning's $405). Olave scored 42.5 in three starts before the chop.
  • It handed Ja'Marr Chase to masonmay. In the same run, GAVINKIRK also bid $740 on Chase, the top bid on him as well. Its Olave claim processed first, and the Chase claim then failed: Sleeper reported a full roster, and it couldn't have afforded both anyway. masonmay got Chase for $426, and he has scored 139.3 starter points for them since.
